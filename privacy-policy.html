Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Facebook Page Poster ("we", "our", or "the Application") handles information when you use our desktop application to manage and post content to your Facebook Pages.

Our Application is designed for personal and business use to simplify content management and posting to Facebook Pages that you own or manage.

2. Information We Collect

Our Application operates entirely on your local device and collects minimal information:

  • Facebook Page Information: Your Facebook Page ID and Page Access Token (stored locally on your device)
  • Content Data: Post content, titles, images, and videos you choose to publish (stored locally)
  • Post History: Records of posts you've published, including timestamps and Facebook post IDs (stored locally in SQLite database)

Important: All data is stored locally on your device. We do not transmit, store, or access your data on any external servers.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used solely to:

  • Authenticate and connect to your Facebook Pages via Facebook Graph API
  • Format and publish content to your Facebook Pages
  • Maintain a local history of your posts for your reference
  • Manage post visibility settings (public/hidden)
  • Schedule posts for future publication

4. Facebook Integration

Our Application integrates with Facebook through the official Facebook Graph API:

  • Permissions Used: pages_manage_posts, pages_read_engagement
  • Data Transmission: Post content, images, and videos are sent directly to Facebook's servers when you publish
  • Access Token: Your Page Access Token is stored locally and used only to authenticate API requests to Facebook
  • Facebook's Privacy Policy: Your interaction with Facebook is governed by Facebook's Privacy Policy

5. Data Storage and Security

  • Local Storage: All application data is stored in a local SQLite database on your device
  • No Cloud Storage: We do not upload, sync, or store your data on any cloud servers
  • Access Control: Only you have access to your local database file
  • Encryption: Access tokens and sensitive data are stored in your local database

Security Recommendation: Keep your Access Tokens confidential and do not share your database file with others.

6. Third-Party Services

Our Application interacts with the following third-party service:

  • Facebook Graph API: Used to publish content to your Facebook Pages. Subject to Facebook Platform Terms

We do not share your data with any other third-party services, analytics platforms, or advertising networks.

7. Data Retention

  • Post history is retained locally until you manually delete it
  • You can clear your post history at any time through the Application
  • Uninstalling the Application will remove all local data from your device

8. Your Rights and Choices

You have full control over your data:

  • Access: You can view all stored data in the Application's management interface
  • Edit: You can edit post content and settings at any time
  • Delete: You can delete individual posts or clear all data
  • Revoke Access: You can remove the Application's access to your Facebook Pages through Facebook Settings

9. Children's Privacy

Our Application is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through our Application, please contact us immediately.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.

11. Data Protection Compliance

Our Application is designed with privacy in mind:

  • No personal data is transmitted to external servers (except Facebook API for posting)
  • No tracking or analytics
  • No advertising or third-party data sharing
  • Complete user control over data
